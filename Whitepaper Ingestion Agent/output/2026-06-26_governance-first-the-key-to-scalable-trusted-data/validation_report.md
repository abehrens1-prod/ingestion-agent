### The Brief (review before publishing)

1. **Only 45.5% of organizations have implemented enterprise-wide governance, creating a critical trust gap in AI analytics.** The 2025 Global AI+BI Survey of 235 organizations found that just 20% allow employees to interact with data using natural language, while 43.4% cite AI hallucinations as their top technical challenge—underscoring how fragmented governance erodes confidence in AI-powered analytics.
2. **A universal semantic layer eliminates inconsistent answers by defining business logic once and applying it consistently across all tools, clouds, and teams.** When metrics like revenue and inventory are governed in a single central semantic model rather than embedded in individual tools, every query—whether from Tableau, Excel, or an AI agent—returns the same result, reducing manual QA overhead and lowering hallucination risk.
3. **Cost predictability is the top analytics goal for the next 12–18 months, cited by 46.4% of organizations, yet 49.0% flag unpredictable costs as a major adoption barrier.** Strategy Mosaic addresses this directly through intelligent query caching, workload portability across clouds and databases, and a per-user pricing model that avoids compute-driven billing surprises—enabling genuine cost arbitrage without sacrificing governance or scale.
