### The Brief (review before publishing)

1. **54% of retail and CPG organizations cite faster decision-making as the top expected outcome from AI-powered analytics.** Yet speed is hampered when BI tools access disjointed and inconsistent data—a problem highlighted by 42% of retailers. Without a consistent semantic layer, trust erodes and AI initiatives struggle to scale.
2. **35% of retail and CPG companies report inaccurate and inconsistent answers—so-called hallucinations—as a top blocker to AI adoption.** These inconsistencies stem from an insufficient semantic layer across hundreds of daily data sources, leading to slower decision-making, erosion of trust, and time-consuming quality assurance before results can be acted on.
3. **53% of retail and CPG respondents cite cost as their primary challenge to scaling AI-powered analytics.** Unexpected compute bills, duplicate modeling, and rigid pricing models undermine the business case for AI+BI. Strategy Mosaic addresses this through intelligent caching, cloud flexibility, and predictable per-user pricing rather than compute-based charges.
